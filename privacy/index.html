MIVLA™ Privacy Policy

Informational Risk Alert System

Version: 1.0 Last updated: 12 May 2026 Effective: Upon app release

Our core privacy commitment: MIVLA analyses content entirely on your device. Images you share with the app are never uploaded to our servers, never transmitted to third parties, and never stored after analysis completes. The AI models that detect generated content run locally inside the app.

2. What MIVLA does

MIVLA helps you assess whether images you encounter on social media are likely to have been generated by artificial intelligence. When you share an image with MIVLA from another app, MIVLA:

  1. Receives the image through the standard Android share mechanism;
  2. Runs on-device analysis using a bundled neural network (CLIP-based detector), optical character recognition (ML Kit Text Recognition, on-device), and metadata inspection;
  3. Displays a result on screen — an AI badge if the content appears generated, and a risk indicator (green / yellow / red) suggesting how cautious you should be about sharing it;
  4. Discards the image and all derived data from memory when you close the result screen.

3. Data MIVLA processes (and does not retain)

The following data is processed temporarily in your device's memory during analysis and is not stored, not transmitted, and not retained after the result is shown:

Data typeSourceHow it is used
Shared image pixelsThe app you shared from (Gallery, Instagram, browser, etc.)Fed to the on-device AI detector for visual analysis
Text extracted from image (OCR)Generated locally by Google ML KitScanned for terms that may indicate generated content
EXIF metadataEmbedded in the image file itselfInspected for camera-provenance signals

This processing happens in volatile memory only. Nothing is written to permanent storage by MIVLA, no copy is retained, and no part of the image or its derived data leaves your device.

4. Data MIVLA does not collect

To be explicit, the following types of data are not collected by MIVLA:

  • Analytics or usage telemetry of any kind;
  • Crash reports sent to third parties;
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data;
  • Location data (neither GPS nor inferred);
  • Contact lists, calendar, microphone, camera, or other system resources beyond what is strictly required to receive shared images;
  • User accounts — MIVLA does not require you to register, sign in, or provide an email address to use the app;
  • Any kind of behavioural profile.

5. Permissions MIVLA requests

Internet

Used solely to communicate with Google Play Billing for managing optional Premium subscriptions. No MIVLA-specific server is contacted; no image or analysis data is transmitted.

Access to shared images

Standard Android permission granted on a per-share basis when you choose MIVLA from the share menu in another app. The permission is scoped to the specific image you share and expires when MIVLA closes.

Future versions

Future versions of MIVLA may request additional permissions (such as Android Accessibility Service for in-app overlay detection on supported platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and X). If and when those features ship, this Privacy Policy will be updated and you will be asked to grant the relevant permission explicitly. No such feature is active in version 1.0.

6. Third-party services

Google Play Billing

If you choose to subscribe to MIVLA Premium, the subscription is processed through Google Play Billing. Google receives the data necessary to complete the purchase (your Google account identifier, payment method, and purchase metadata) according to Google's Privacy Policy. MIVLA receives only an entitlement token from Google confirming that you have an active subscription — no payment details, no account information, no personal identifiers.

No other third parties

MIVLA does not integrate any other third-party SDK, analytics platform, advertising network, crash-reporting service, or remote configuration system in version 1.0.

7. Your rights under GDPR

Because MIVLA does not collect or retain personal data, most data-subject rights under the GDPR (Articles 15–22) have a limited application here — there is, in practical terms, no data on file for us to access, rectify, port, or erase. Nevertheless, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we process personal data about you (we do not);
  • Object to any future processing that we might introduce;
  • Withdraw any consent you may have given at any time;
  • Lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence — in Portugal, this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@xtremeprojects.org. We respond within 30 days as required by Article 12 of the GDPR.

8. Children

MIVLA is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the higher minimum age set by applicable law in your jurisdiction, including 16 years in some EU member states under GDPR Article 8). We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you become aware that a minor has been using the app in a manner that requires intervention, contact us at info@xtremeprojects.org.

9. Security

Because MIVLA processes all content locally and does not transmit data to any server, the attack surface is intentionally minimal. There is no MIVLA database to breach, no MIVLA account to compromise, no MIVLA backend to intercept. The neural network model bundled with the app is protected by standard Android application sandboxing and Play Integrity verification.

10. International data transfers

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data, no international data transfers occur in the operation of MIVLA. If you subscribe through Google Play Billing, transfers related to that transaction are governed by Google's own privacy policy and standard contractual clauses.
